Got Questions?
We've Got Answers

What is Skill Swap and how does it work? ▾

SkillSwap is a peer learning platform where students can offer skills they're good at and learn new ones from fellow students. Simply create a profile, list your skills, browse others, and book a session!

Is it free to join and use the platform? ▾

Yes, Skill Swap is completely free to use. Just sign up, create your profile, and start connecting with learners and teachers.

How do I find the right person to swap skills with? ▾

Use our search and filter system to browse by category, skill level, availability, and rating. You can also post requests on the Community Board.

What types of skills can I exchange? ▾

Anything from programming and design to languages, music, cooking, fitness, and academic subjects. If you can teach it, you can list it!
